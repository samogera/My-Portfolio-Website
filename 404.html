About

I'm a Nairobi-based founder who writes code. I believe the next wave of African tech will be infrastructure, not just apps: systems that move materials, money and energy in places where the formal economy doesn't reach yet.

I trained as a software engineer at Moringa School and I'm studying Entrepreneurial Leadership at African Leadership University. What I value most is going out to meet the people I'm building for. The scrap-yard owner in Ongata Rongai didn't ask for blockchain. He asked for his business to run more smoothly, and that's the standard I build to.
